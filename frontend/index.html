Physicians in the loop

Every signature makes the next case better.

Approved and rejected reports become preference pairs. The model is retrained when you choose, then benchmarked against the base model before it goes back on the ward.

  • Graded, not just approvedDiagnosis, reasoning, workup and treatment safety are scored separately, with a star rating and notes.
  • A dataset you can inspectOpen the preference dataset and see exactly which judgements shaped the model.
  • Benchmarked before releaseEach new policy is compared with the base model on diagnostic accuracy, test choice and contraindication avoidance.
