Virtual Hospital (AI-VH) System Architecture

Publication-grade method figure designed for CCF-A proceedings (CVPR / ICLR / NeurIPS format)
Figure 1: Architectural framework of the AI-VH Autonomous Clinical Deliberation & Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) System. (a) Clinical Case Intake & MedGemma Triage Router: Patient EHR data $x$ (intake demographics, vitals, labs, imaging) is encoded and dispatched via the MedGemma routing network $\mathcal{M}_{\text{router}}$ into 7 departmental specialist agents (Internal Medicine, Cardiology, Neurology, Surgery, Emergency Medicine, Obstetrics, Gynecology) using active/standby state management. (b) Multi-Agent Deliberation & Grounded RAG: Active specialists perform retrieval-augmented reasoning over indexed medical knowledge bases (Harrison’s, Braunwald’s, Williams, Schwartz’s) and real-time pharmacology APIs (RxNorm, openFDA), which the NVIDIA Nemotron synthesis engine deliberates to produce a candidate Clinical Assessment Report $y$. (c) Attending Physician Attestation & Multi-Dimensional Grading: Human physicians evaluate candidate reports across four clinical axes (Diagnosis, Reasoning, Workup, Safety), partitioning candidate reports into chosen ($y_w$) and rejected ($y_l$) pairs within preference dataset $\mathcal{D}_{\text{pref}}$. (d) On-Device DPO Alignment & Closed-Loop Feedback: Using parameter-efficient LoRA on local GPU hardware, the policy model $\pi_\theta$ is continuously optimized against the frozen reference model $\pi_{\text{ref}}$ via the DPO objective $\mathcal{L}_{\text{DPO}}$, routing improved weights back into the clinical ward in an active closed-loop cycle.

Panel (a): Intake & MedGemma Router

Encodes multi-modal inpatient admission telemetry $x$ including objective ESI acuity scores. Computes domain dispatch probabilities across 7 specialist agents, managing initial Ready state, standby partitioning, and dynamic handoff referrals.

Panel (b): Multi-Agent Deliberation & RAG

Specialists ground clinical assertions in authoritative medical literature with verbatim chapter and page citations. The NVIDIA Nemotron-3-Nano-30B-A3B engine synthesizes differential diagnoses, diagnostic orders, and management plans.

Panel (c): Human Attestation & Dataset Generation

Attending physicians conduct a single-click attestation or submit multi-dimensional clinical grades. Approved reports and clinician corrections are paired to form verified DPO preference triplets $(x, y_w, y_l)$.

Panel (d): On-Device DPO Fine-Tuning

Executes Direct Preference Optimization without external reward model instability, comparing $\pi_\theta$ against $\pi_{\text{ref}}$ on local RTX GPU infrastructure, benchmarked across 6 clinical axes.

$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{DPO}}(\pi_\theta; \pi_{\text{ref}}) = -\mathbb{E}_{(x, y_w, y_l) \sim \mathcal{D}_{\text{pref}}} \left[ \log \sigma \left( \beta \log \frac{\pi_\theta(y_w|x)}{\pi_{\text{ref}}(y_w|x)} - \beta \log \frac{\pi_\theta(y_l|x)}{\pi_{\text{ref}}(y_l|x)} \right) \right]$$
