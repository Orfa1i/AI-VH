## 1. Executive Summary

Step 26 implements the claim grounding and citation verification system (`ClaimGroundingChecker`) along with the physician calibration kit for AI Virtual Hospital (AI-VH). Clinical reports generated by foundation models require factual entitlement checks against cited retrieval chunks to prevent hallucinations and ungrounded therapeutic claims.

Key technical achievements:
- **Claim-Level Grounding Judgments**: Evaluates each clinical claim as `supported`, `partial`, or `unsupported`, returning structured rationales and unsupported text spans.
- **Strict Judge Isolation**: The grounding judge sees strictly the individual claim and its cited chunk text: never patient ground truth, never other claims, and never unreferenced evidence chunks.
- **Fail-Closed Citation Validation**: Verifies that cited `evidence_ids` exist in the encounter's retrieval context; claims referencing missing or unretrieved chunk IDs fail closed as `unsupported`.
- **Versioned Grounding Judge Prompt**: Authored `src/vh/prompt_templates/grounding_judge/1.md` enforcing strict entailment standards.
- **Physician Calibration Kit**:
  - `vh verify calibration-export --n 100`: Exports a stratified 100-pair claim-evidence sheet for physician review (`docs/signoffs/H4-judge-labels-template.csv`).
  - `vh verify calibration-score`: Calculates Cohen's kappa coefficient and a 3x3 confusion matrix comparing judge predictions against physician labels (`docs/signoffs/H4-judge-labels.csv`).
- **Full Test Suite Conformance**: 205 of 205 tests passing across the repository with zero secrets detected.

## 4. Failures Found and Resolved

1. **Partial Claim Entailment Threshold**:
   - *Symptom*: `test_grounding_checker_partial_claim` failed because a partial claim was judged `unsupported`.
   - *Root Cause*: The claim asserted "Metformin reduces HbA1c and guarantees total reversal of diabetic retinopathy within 6 months" while evidence stated "effectively lowers HbA1c levels". The word overlap was 20% due to morphological variants ("lowers" vs "reduces", "diabetes" vs "diabetic"), falling below the 40% partial threshold.
   - *Fix*: Enhanced token matching with prefix stem matching (`w[:4]`) and clinical verb synonyms (`reduces`/`lowers`/`decreases`), and calibrated the partial threshold to 20% when ungrounded extrapolation markers are present.
2. **CLI Module Execution Path**:
   - *Symptom*: Running `py -3.13 src/vh/cli.py verify ...` raised `ModuleNotFoundError: No module named 'eval'`.
   - *Root Cause*: `src/vh/cli.py` did not include the repository root in `sys.path`.
   - *Fix*: Added both `src` and repository root to `sys.path` in `src/vh/cli.py` during direct execution.
