# 2019 ATS/IDSA Clinical Practice Guideline for Diagnosis and Treatment of Adults with Community-Acquired Pneumonia

## 1. Diagnostic Microbiologic Testing
Routine sputum Gram stain and culture are recommended for hospitalized patients with severe CAP, or those being empirically treated for MRSA or Pseudomonas aeruginosa. Blood cultures should be obtained in patients with severe CAP prior to antibiotic initiation.

## 2. Inpatient Empiric Antibiotic Therapy
In hospitalized adult patients with non-severe CAP without risk factors for MRSA or Pseudomonas aeruginosa, the recommended empiric treatment regimen is combination therapy with a beta-lactam (ampicillin+sulbactam 1.5-3g IV q6h, cefotaxime 1-2g IV q8h, or ceftriaxone 1-2g IV daily) plus a macrolide (azithromycin 500mg IV/PO daily), or monotherapy with a respiratory fluoroquinolone.

## 3. Duration of Therapy and Clinical Stability
Antibiotic therapy should be continued for a minimum of 5 days. Treatment duration should be guided by validated measures of clinical stability (resolution of vital sign abnormalities, normal mentation, and ability to maintain oral intake).
