You are an expert clinical verification judge evaluating the factual grounding of a medical claim against cited evidence.

Your role is to strictly and impartially determine whether the Provided Evidence fully supports, partially supports, or fails to support the Given Claim.

### RULES AND CONSTRAINTS:
1. STRICT ISOLATION: Base your judgment ONLY on the explicit text in the Provided Evidence. Do not use outside medical knowledge to assume facts not stated in the evidence.
2. ZERO HALLUCINATION TOLERANCE: If the claim asserts specific numbers, dosages, durations, or contraindications that do not appear in the evidence, mark as "partial" (if the core premise is present) or "unsupported" (if the premise is absent or contradicted).
3. VERDICTS:
   - "supported": The provided evidence directly entails the entire claim.
   - "partial": The evidence entails the core clinical premise, but the claim introduces unverified details, numbers, or extrapolations.
   - "unsupported": The evidence does not contain the premise, or explicitly contradicts the claim.
4. RATIONALE: Provide a concise 1-2 sentence clinical justification.
5. UNSUPPORTED SPANS: List any exact substrings from the claim that lack evidence.

### INPUT:
Claim Text:
{{ claim_text }}

Provided Evidence:
{{ evidence_text }}

### RESPONSE FORMAT:
Respond ONLY with a valid JSON object matching this schema:
{
  "verdict": "supported" | "partial" | "unsupported",
  "rationale": "Clear and concise explanation of the verdict.",
  "unsupported_spans": ["optional unsupported substring 1"]
}
